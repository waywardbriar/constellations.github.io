Senexoth Role Feats

You are one of many individuals that buzz about the island home of one of the Consecrated families. Whether your role is as small as a fly on the wall, or you’re a direct member of the family - all make an effort on Pthalo to be the best of the best. Pthalo is a prestigious family with more than thirty members - directly serving under the current Queen Mirador.

In addition to your background and other feats, you may select of one of the following roles: Not The Chosen One, Servant, Chamberlain, Chancellor, Constable, Jester, Chaplain, Falconer. You cannot overlap with other players and should make an effort to discuss amongst yourselves which role fits who best.

Feat Source Description
Consecrated Regalia Not The Chosen One You may, or may not be the chosen one. As such, people treat you differently. Opposing enclaves may treat you more poorly, while other civilians may treat you closer to a deity.
Behind The Curtains Servant Your station in society is to act as a pillar to the minutiae that others take for granted. Few people notice you, some will refuse to even acknowledge you.
Entanglement Chamberlain You spend hours pouring over details, time in the books, keeping schedules and making contacts. Any time you would meet an individual or person with a connection to a governmental position, you can learn one interesting thing about them.
To Arms! Chancellor You have a way with words, and knowing certain penal codes. Once a day you can call on any individual using these codes to assist you in a task, whether that is battle or some other task.
En Guarde Constable Years spent training as protector and enforcer have leant you many skills the average person doesn’t have. You can take an additional two Combat Expertise in different fighting styles free.
I Jest, Good Fellow Jester Any time you personally would have an unsuccessful bid during a social encounter, you can instead play it off as a joke. It will not negatively impact the outcome of the conversation.
Cup Bearer Chaplain As an astute member of the Ecclesia Avscein, you have privileged access to history, secrets, and locations that most in society will never see or hear of. Once a day you can invoke the tenants of Avscein to gain access to a location, make appointments, collect an item or other items as appropriate.
Companion Falconer You have a special bond with a creature of your choosing, within reason. You can understand each other. You can give them commands, and they will do their best to follow it.
