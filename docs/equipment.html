Equipment

Players can find possible equipment below and the rules for such items.

Keep in mind that there is a difference in inventory and equipped items. Players can generally only have a specific amount of items equipped and active and must spend time to change such items.

The Equipment Tree

Each part of the body can only hold one specific type of equipment at a time, unless a feat specifies otherwise. Accessories, on the other hand, have no general limit. (within reason) The sections of the body include:

Head, Torso, Arms, Hands, Legs, Feet, Accessories

You also have an Over and Under slot for items specifically labeled as to be worn under or over others.

Any wearable equipment will list its wear conditions and what slots on the tree it fills. Some may not count, others may count for only one, and a few may count towards multiple slots.

You can only have a maximum of 6 Guard altogether from the armor you are wearing, even if it adds to more without special rulings, feats or spells.

Item Conditions

Some items may degrade or be impacted by forces in the world. The possible conditions are listed below. Item conditions do not have magnitudes; they apply a flat penalty to the use of the object. It is also possible in rare cases for the quality of the object to be impacted if not repaired in a timely manner. Items can be repaired with the proper time, materials, and feats. You can also find services to repair items in the world. An item can only carry 1 condition at a time; if an item suffers another condition while it already has one, it is considered broken instead.

Condition Description Penalty Tags
Rusted The metal components of the items have accrued rust and don’t move as well. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Rust,
Cracked A crack has compromised the structure. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Cracked,
Broken The item has broken into multiple pieces. Unusable Degradation, Condition, Items, Broken,
Dull The edge of the item has become dulled. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Dull,
Overgrown A plant has overtaken the pieces and parts of the device. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Overgrown,
Rotted The structure has begun to rot and needs to be replaced or discarded. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Rotted,
Missing Piece A part of the device went missing, causing it to malfunction. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Missing Piece,
Jammed Something is stuck, and needs to be unstuck. -1 Degradation, Condition, Items, Jammed, Ranged
Frayed The fabric or the things holding the fibers are breaking down. -1 Degradation, Fabric, Condition, Items

Weapons

A special note should be made for weapons. They essentially act as an addition to an attack bid. Some may only add 1; others more. All weapons have an effective range, though penalties are typically inflicted for being outside this range unless noted by the GM.

Additionally, to use a weapon effectively, you should be trained in said weapon - training comes from the Combat Expertise feat, and specific weapons need special training.

Ranged weapons have a maximum range, and an effective range. This means that the weapons do more damage within a certain range, less in maximum range, and even less outside both.

Reach weapons are only effective at their reach distance unless noted. Their penalty is similar to ranged weapons; in this way, anything outside their range is at a distinct disadvantage.

All weapons are generalized in a way that players are highly encouraged to expand on the weapon they choose; a sword could look any way, same with any other weapon. Name the weapon, select a specific type of sword, etc. The design is in your hands.

As you know already, there are three types of damage; most weapons will only do physical damage. A few will do energy damage, which broadly refers to anything that has been altered with resonance or technology in a special way. These will be denoted in the damage type column.

Lodging is priced per night, and living is priced per month.
