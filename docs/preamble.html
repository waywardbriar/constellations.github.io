Preamble

Each morning a new star, new life. Ships adrift in the cosmos. The wayfarers whispered life into each corner, blessing them with a sip from their chalice.

When they found our shores, it would be the last stop on their journey. Their ship was knocked from the sky by the great belt circling the planet. Their people tried to rebuild and tried to leave, but the planet kept them here. Striking their rafts and their boats with hot iron from those rings.

Among them, the one who carried the chalice spilled their cup, the cosmos filling the land in deep rivers. Those who drank of the banks of the starlit river became more, felt more, and knew more. These would be our ancestors, the first of the Consecrated; the ones who would build the cornerstones of our society.
