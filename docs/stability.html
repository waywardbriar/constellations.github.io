Stability

There is magic rife in this world, flowing between the creatures, the land, and the plants. It’s a mystery's balance and changes everything it touches. Occasionally these forces can warp and alter things in unexpected ways. Everyone has various levels of susceptibility to these changes; how people react to them is highly dependent on the culture. This could be an extra horn, a couple of years of age, hooves, hair, or even a change in eye color.

Derived from your Soul stat. Your total Stability is equal to 1 + ½ your Soul stat, rounded up. You will use this when the situation calls for it; this will act as a bonus to any bid you make against resisting increasing corruption.

To avoid corruption, you must bid for any number of actions, including, but not limited to:

  • Repeated casting of a spell over a short duration

  • Casting a powerful spell with multiple components

  • Entering an area that has been corrupted or is out of magical balance

  • Spending prolonged time with contaminated objects

  • Encountering unusual creatures

  • Getting hit by certain spells and abilities.
