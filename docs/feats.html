Feats

Feats are at the heart of this game; they determine your knowledge, your abilities, and how you interact with the world around you. You can be actively selecting feats from multiple trees or specialize in one. Some feats provide specific situational advantages; others may help you more generally. It’s up to you to determine who you are.

There are seven feat trees to select from. Each feat tree has four tiers. The tiers generally all cost the same, except for a few selections. You may also notice a background section; this contains feats from events in the story, to your selected background.

Early feats represent basic training and your personal experience with something. It reflects your style and competence. As you progress through each tree, you gain more abilities, the power to modify rolls, bend rules, and create your own specific style of play.

A feat must be unlocked in a lower tier before selecting feats from a higher tier. The Feat Tiers available at character creation are restricted by your Start Type. You may also only have access to certain tiers at the start of character creation, be sure to reference the Starting Merit Table or your GM.

It’s important to note that not having a specific feat does not mean you cannot do something, merely that you are unskilled. The GM may then invoke penalties for your actions rather than saying you cannot do something.

The trees can be found and browsed on the following pages. The seven trees are Combat, Mobility, Survival, Social, Cognition, Craftsmanship & Magic.

Untrained

You might inquire - why do I need this feat? A feat represents a specialization or a boost to an aspect of yourself. Some are more specifically stat-focused, others combat-focused, and some are skills-focused. While it is always possible to attempt a skill without the accompanying knowledge, you will have far more difficulty. Attempting something your character has no training in will result in taking the Untrained penalty during any bid attempting the activity.

Retraining

You can retrain a feat, given the appropriate amount of time to do so. Higher level feats should reasonably take more time to retrain. At GM's discretion, you can refund and redistribute merit. This shouldn’t be done on a whim and should represent an important departure or course change in your character’s journey.

Combat

While hopefully unnecessary in the day-to-day, some people believe that knowing how to defend yourself is as important as anything else. Combat skills and feats relate to anything that might see you in the line of fire, strategizing and engaging in battle. They improve your abilities with different forms of combat as well as how efficient you are on the battlefield.
