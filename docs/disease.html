Disease

Throughout the worlds, there are a number of disease of different types, which can affect your interactions with others and ability to do certain actions in the day to day. These are represented below and all have magnitudes similar to some conditions and injuries as you’ve become familiar with them.

Disease Table
Name Effects
Iraventus (Rain Lung) Relatively common, transmitted via fluids. Causes coughing, body aches and dehydration.
Fulgallis (Static) Relatively common, transmitted via contaminated surfaces. Causes debilitating rashes which have a specific branching pattern.
Senius (Bastard’s Rot) A variety of necrotizing bacteria which enters soft tissue causing sores and possible necrotic patches.
Vendespuo (VDP) Causes intense stomach pain, intestinal distress and high fever. Highly contagious.
Amissen (Violist’s Bane) Transmitted via particulates in the air. A serious condition which attacks the nervous system. In minor severity, it causes numbness, while more serious cases can attack the brain and whole limb function.
Gena A disease transmitted by insects of differing types. Causes profuse sweating, disorientation, and mild hallucinations at higher severity.
Vox A unique disease that attacks the vocal cords and throat, can cause loss of voice temporarily or permanently.
Aurertum (Thresher’s Mark) A disease marked by a gold rash, the rash spreads causing damage to muscles and connective tissue.
Traveler’s Woe A disease which is spread via various wildlife. The conditions of which largely depend on the species of creature it was attained from. Generally the disease causes confusion, spontaneous amelioration, and irritability.
Verdure Noticable by patches of plant materials and other things beginning to grow on a persons body, this often ends in them being completely consumed by plant materials.
Null Causes severe exhaustion, infected are usually unable to channel without complications.
Thinned Makes the infected more vulnerable to background magic and more likely to be afflicted by strange bodily alterations and other changes.
Slipped Cuases the infected to somehow be dislodged from the normal flow of time, whether they experience it slower of faster than the average person is highly dependent on the case.
