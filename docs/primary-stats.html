Primary Stats

Outside your stamina, other smaller aspects of your personhood and interactions with the world will be affected by the three primary stats gone over below. These may seem inconsequential, but they make up the core of your character. Each affects many things outside itself that might not be immediately obvious; these are gone over below and how each primary stat affects your secondary abilities.

The total max for each stat is 12, with stats being upgraded by spending merit between sessions or at rest. The cost of buying a point depends on how many you already have in a stat. The table for these purchases is below.

Mind

In totality, mind encompasses knowledge, awareness, reasoning, and other related things. It’s your ability to think through problems and also maintain your sense of self.

  • Reputation: this is people's natural perception of you based on your actions in and around the world. In addition to sentiment, it can affect how certain interactions with different people will go. (= 1+ Mind / 2 Rounded Down)

  • Scope: The number of spells you can know and have active at any time. (= 1 + Mind)

  • Poise: your resistance to social magic and undue influence and how effective your social interactions are. (=1 + Mind / 2 Rounded Up)

Body

This represents your character's physical form and capabilities. This includes your strength, speed, and reaction time. As well as resistance to certain maladies and effects.

  • Stamina: How much energy you have to expend through a day before you need to rest. (5 + Body)

  • Injury Slots: The amount of damage you can take before incapacitation or death. (2 + Body)

  • Tempo: How fast you react and move through the world. (= 2 + Body / 2 Rounded Up)

Soul

This is you, the force of your person. One could describe it as will, presence, ambition, and persistence. Soul helps keep your character grounded and themselves. It resists magic’s corruptions and helps you stay you.

  • Grit: Directly affects your ability to recover during rest as well as recover from certain conditions over time. (= 1 + Soul / 2 Rounded Down)

  • Stability: Determines your ability to resist the influence of magical corruption. (1 + Soul / 2 Rounded Up)

  • Exertion: Determines the maximum amount of effort you can expend from your stamina on any single bid. (Soul + 2)

Increasing Primary Stats

Throughout gameplay you will garner merit, through your deeds and actions, selling crafts, and other tasks. You will learn more about merit specifically later on. As you gather merit though, you can spend it to improve your gear, or in this case your self. The cost of Stat points (Mind, Body, Soul) is laid out along with the merit it costs to increase at each level below.
