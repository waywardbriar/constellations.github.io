Stamina & The Bidding System

Stamina

To play the game you will need a series of D8 dice, these will represent your stamina as a character. Starting out, you will begin with an amount equivalent to 5 + Body Stat. To utilize stamina, you choose a skill or action, and roll a number of dice according to the skill or predetermined by the GM. This represents the effort exerted to complete an action. Some actions are more complicated and may require more dice.

Rolling 8’s represent strain, and will cause you to temporarily lose a stamina dice until you rest or recover.

For example, to utilize a certain skill you must utilize 3 dice, if any of the dice rolled are an eight, you lose a die from your stamina pool.

Recovery & Resting

Your ability to recover depends on the amount of time you rest, as well as your Grit Stat. There are a few types of rest, Breathers, Rest, & Recovery.

Breathers

Rest

Recovery
Generally understood as less than three hours but as short as thirty minutes. Breathers represent you treating any minor wounds, recovering from some conditions and other related activities. Generally understood as taking an entire evening and some hours to sleep, rest and recoup. Usually a minimum of seven hours, a maximum of twelve hours. Generally understood as taking several days to completely recover, typically a week. This is more involved and usually allows you to recover from multiple injuries and treat various conditions. This is the only way to heal major injuries without outside intervention.

  • Recoup Stamina Equal to your Grit Stat

  • Remove One Minor Condition or Injury

  • Recoup All Stamina

  • Remove injuries or conditions with combined magnitude of up to 2 + ½ Grit rounding up, Cannot heal Major Injuries.

  • Recoup All Stamina

  • Remove All Minor and Moderate Injuries.

  • Remove A Number of Major Injuries equivalent to your grit stat.

Bidding

Behind every basic mechanic and at the heart of the game is the bidding system. In that across the board all dice rolls are essentially the same.

You will utilize your dice pool (your Stamina) and determine the number of dice you are willing to while risk completing an action. Your maximum stamina is equivalent to your Body Stat + 5.

You do have a limiter on how many die you are allowed to spend on any one action/bid at a time. This is called Exertion and is determined by your Soul stat which is gone over later. This doesn’t include any bonuses from feats or other things.

The action of rolling your die is called bidding. This can be moving in combat, attacking, casting a spell, tinkering on a machine, etc. Any action that uses some modicum of skill will require bidding, especially early on.

Think about bidding as effort put into an action. Actions you excel at will require lower effort, and those that you aren’t as skill at will require more effort.

You are effectively choosing how many dice you want to use and rolling said dice. The die roll is to see if you incur any strain from your efforts, meaning rolling an 8 means you are strained while performing an action and loose one stamina per 8 from your pool until you manage to rest.

Rolling multiple eights can result in a complication in completing an action, and in certain cases, failure. Complications generally are narrative, but on occasion can affect the outcome of a check in unexpected ways.

Rolling 1 or multiple 1’s represents your character having focus, clarity or a certain amount of luck.

Each one allows you to add +1 to an effect, or allow you a small advantage during story, clearing minor conditions, gaining social leverage. This will largely be determined by the scenario and your GM.

Any roll between 2-7 on a die represents a good effort on your character's part and doesn’t overly affect success or failure of a roll.

Loosing all of your stamina leaves your character Fatigued and generally unable to take actions without risk to themselves. Certain abilities let you push past this limit.

With that in mind you aren’t rolling to hit a number, but rather to see how your energy is spent. The only goal is to bid higher than the effort, or guard required by the opposing force.

Furthermore, you will not know how many dice you need to roll to win a bid unless you have a special feat that allows you to know such information.

For example:

Essentially say you are trying to pick a lock,

  1. Make your bid, (choose how much effort to expend, or how many die from your stamina pool you will risk)

  2. The action has a hidden challenge rating which is equivalent to a number of dice which your GM knows.

  3. You will roll your dice to see if you incur any strain from your action.

  4. You bid 5 die, and have a bonus of 1 from a feat, so a total of six die. This is over the difficulty known only by the GM( in this case it was 3) which means you are successful in your action.

  5. However, you rolled an eight, which means you lose stamina until your next rest.

Roll Effect
Multiple 8s X Strain + Story Complication: Any event which can affect you or your party narratively in a negative fashion.
8 1 Strain Per 8 Rolled
2-7 No effect/Normal Effort
1 1 Story Opportunity: Any event or opportunity granted to you or your party for actions carried out with great skill and finesse.

Effort Pooling - Assistance

At any point you can choose to act as a group rather than an individual when it comes to completing tasks. This is usually on a case by case basis where it makes sense narratively - but you can choose to assist others by allowing them to utilize any number of dice from your stamina pool. These dice are considered used and subtracted from your pool until rest.

There is a risk in this that any strain accumulated by the primary party affects anybody that assisted as well in addition to assistance dice.

You can choose to assist in combat to guard or otherwise if you have reasonable opportunity.
