Health - Injury Slots

Health is determined by your body start and is represented as Injury Slots. To determine your starting health, you will add your Body Stat +2.

Each slot represents a degree of physical damage to your character, and some may come with specific effects/feats that affect other aspects of play.

It is important to note that injuries are separate from stamina. If you fill all of your injury slots, your character reaches Fatigue automatically and is considered Incapacitated, and in some cases, could die.

Injuries

Injuries are specified by you or your target usually. Where you hit, though, is more of a specific feat and skill you gain over time and training. The type of damage depends on the weapon or what caused the injury in the first place. Most all weapons do physical damage, while spells do elemental damage.

Injuries vary in magnitude from 1 to 4, representing Mild, Moderate, Major and Fatal. Most injuries will be mild and moderate, with a rare few crossing the threshold into Major and Fatal.

When adding an injury to your injury track, you will include the injury name, any notes, the body part affected, and any linked conditions.

Each injury will show up as a card and describe what the injury is and its severity. In addition, injuries introduce penalties to your maximum stamina that lasts as long as you have the injury.

*Death Complete cessation of life.

On Death

Players should have an honest discussion before the game on their expectations of how things should work during gameplay. For the best possible outcome, the GM and players should agree on how serious or often deaths should occur.

Things to consider should include, how deadly is combat and whether death is permenant or not. As is, the system has feats and abilities that can be included or excluded based on these discussions.
