Resistance

Resistance exists as a feat that is added to certain characters and players depending on the scenario. A resistance will take the form of: Resistance X. Where X represents a number of die. The type of resistance is mentioned by the feat. This means for each time a player guards against some attack, environmental factor, or social situation of the resistance type, a player's guard or bid is treated as X higher than they actually bid.

For example:

A creature has Resistance 2: Physical

A player makes a physical attack against them, the player bidding 3 die.

If the creature only bids 2 die to guard, then it technically counts as 4 die, and where they would normally lose the bid, they instead win.
